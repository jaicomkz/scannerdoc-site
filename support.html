Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, open a support request.

Smart Summary or Ask doesn't work

Smart features run on Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. They need a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri. Right after you turn it on, the model may still be downloading.

My documents don't appear on my other devices

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices and that iCloud is turned on for Scanner Doc in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. The app's Settings screen shows the sync status.

How do I scan from another app?

Use the Share button in Mail, Files or Safari and choose Scanner Doc. The file appears in your library the next time you open the app.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Scanner Doc, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone and choose Scanner Doc. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I forgot how to open a locked document

Locked documents open with Face ID or your iPhone passcode. Nobody else, including us, can unlock them.
