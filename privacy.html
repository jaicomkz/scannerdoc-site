Scanner Doc

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Scanner Doc reads your documents on your iPhone. We don't run servers that receive your scans, and we don't collect personal data.

What stays on your device

Scanned pages, recognized text, summaries, answers to your questions, signatures, form-filling details and expenses are created and stored on your device. Text recognition, document sorting and smart features use Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision, Natural Language, Core ML and Apple Intelligence). Your documents are not sent to us or to any third party for processing.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on for Scanner Doc, your documents sync between your devices through your private iCloud database. Apple stores this data under your Apple Account. We cannot access it. You can turn this off in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents and read text live. Images are used only inside the app.
  • Photos: only when you import pictures. You choose which ones.
  • Face ID: to lock the app or individual documents. Apple handles authentication; the app never sees your face data.
  • Notifications: to remind you about deadlines you choose. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

Analytics and advertising

Scanner Doc contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no tracking. If you opt in to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may share anonymous crash data with us.

Sharing

Documents leave the app only when you share or export them, for example as a PDF, image or CSV file, using the share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app to remove it from your device and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes all local data.

Children

Scanner Doc is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
